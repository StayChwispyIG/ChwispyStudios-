    Cancellation and rescheduling

    • Your 50% booking retainer reserves your date, which I then turn down other work for, and covers planning.
    • Reschedule once for free with at least 48 hours’ notice, or 7 days for events. Your retainer moves to the new date. New dates depend on availability.
    • Later changes: if you reschedule inside that window, or a second time, a $150 rescheduling fee applies.
    • You may cancel at any time. How much of your retainer is kept depends on notice:
      • 30+ days before: I keep 25% of the package price for reserving your date and planning. The rest of your retainer is refunded or kept as credit, your choice.
      • Under 30 days: your retainer is kept, since the date usually can’t be rebooked. You can use it as credit toward a new date within 12 months.
      • Inside 48 hours, or a no-show: your retainer is kept.
    • Weather or safety: if an outdoor shoot isn’t safe or usable, we’ll move it at no charge.
    • If I have to cancel: a new date at no cost, or a full refund including your retainer, your choice.
    • Websites and design: if you cancel mid-project, you pay for the work completed at $75/hr. Your retainer is applied, and any difference is refunded or billed.
    • Monthly packages: these have a 3-month minimum. After that, cancel any time with 30 days’ notice by replying to any invoice or messaging me.
