AboutVideographer, director, designer

Behind the lens

I’m Chris, most people call me Chwispy. I make video for brands that need it to work, not just look good.

One person, the whole picture

Most brands juggle a shooter, an editor, a designer and a social person. I’m all four.

Day to day, I lead the website, social media and brand content for an aftermarket performance company. I shoot it, edit it and run the accounts. That means I see what happens after a video goes live: what gets watched, what gets shared, and what actually sells.

I also manage the brand’s influencer and sponsorship partnerships, so I know what brands need from content and what a creator needs to deliver it.

I design too. Visual identity, thumbnails, on-screen graphics. When I make your video, the titles, the cover frame and the look all come from the same hands, so everything matches.

Before all this, I was a senior martial arts instructor leading a team of instructors. That’s where I learned to stay calm under pressure, keep a room focused, and get people to relax and perform. It’s the most useful skill I bring to set.

What I bring

In-house content lead

I shoot, edit and run content for an aftermarket performance brand every day. Your project gets the same pace and judgment.

Partnerships

I manage influencer and sponsorship deals, so I build content that fits campaigns and performs on social.

Design

Identity, thumbnails and motion graphics, done by me. One look across every frame.

Founder

Director

Years of leading instructors and classes taught me how to direct people who aren’t actors and make them look natural.

Concept to delivery

  1. Listen

    We start with your goal and your audience, not a shot list.

  2. Plan

    You get a treatment and schedule to approve. No guessing on shoot day.

  3. Direct

    I run a calm, quick set. Your team won’t feel like they’re acting.

  4. Finish

    Edit, color, sound and graphics in-house, with clear revision rounds.

  5. Launch

    Files sized for every platform, plus notes on how to post them.
