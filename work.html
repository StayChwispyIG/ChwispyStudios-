Pop Clutch Coffee

A coffee cart inside Good Trouble Supply Co. in Redlands needed one page that tells people what’s pouring, when it’s open and how to get there.

  • One fast page designed around their own yellow and black look, with checkered race-flag touches
  • Menu with Signature and Classic tabs, plus a live open or closed status
  • Hours, directions, gift cards and rewards links, and a founders story
  • Phone layout with a bottom bar for menu, directions and hours
  • A matching Squarespace kit so they can rebuild it on their own platform
Designed for Pop Clutch as a concept
NAVI Media
WebsiteVideo and photo, Inland Empire

NAVI Media

An Inland Empire videographer needed a site that shows the work, explains the packages and turns a visitor into a booked date.

  • Black, metallic gold and grey look with their logo in the header and footer
  • Instagram reels and YouTube films embedded right on the page
  • Pricing tabs for monthly content, productions, day rates and photo
  • Booking calendar: pick a date, package and place, and the request goes straight to the owner
  • Review form that only takes reviews with the owner’s code, shown once approved
