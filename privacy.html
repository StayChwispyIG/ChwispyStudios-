Who I am

Chwispy Studio is a one-person studio run by Chris (Chwispy) in the Inland Empire, Southern California. This policy covers chwispystudio.com. Questions about it go through the Start a project page or @chwispystudio on Instagram.

The quote form

The Start a project form asks what you need, your goal, your name and your phone number, plus an optional note. When you tap Send, the site emails your answers to my studio inbox through FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), a form-to-email service, which keeps a copy for 30 days. Nothing is sent before you tap Send. If it can’t go through, the form opens the message in your own email app instead, and that only reaches me if you hit send there.

Once you send it, I keep your message in my email so I can reply, put together your quote and keep a record of the project.

Analytics and session replays

If you accept cookies, the site uses PostHog to show me how it’s used, so I can fix what doesn’t work. PostHog records:

  • Pages you view, buttons and links you click, and the site that sent you here
  • Your browser, device type, screen size and rough location, which PostHog works out from your IP address
  • Replays of how you move through pages. Anything typed into a field is hidden, and the quote form’s name, phone and note are never recorded
  • When you finish the quote form: only the service and goal you picked, never your name, number or note

PostHog stores this data in the United States. If you decline, PostHog never loads.

Cookies and storage

The site saves a few small things in your browser:

  • Your light or dark mode choice, so the site remembers it
  • Your cookie choice, so the banner doesn’t keep asking
  • PostHog’s cookie and visitor ID, only if you accept

You can change your mind any time with , which is also at the bottom of every page. Declining after accepting stops PostHog and clears what it saved.

Other services the site uses

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. Like any web host, it handles your IP address to deliver the pages and may keep it in its server logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the site’s fonts, so your browser asks Google for them and Google sees your IP address.
  • Instagram links open Instagram, which has its own privacy policy.

There are no advertising trackers on this site, and I don’t sell or share your information for advertising.

Your choices

  • Turn analytics on or off with Cookie settings.
  • Ask me what information I have from you, or ask me to delete it, through the Start a project page or Instagram.
  • Block cookies in your browser. The site still works without them.

Children

This site is for businesses and adults. It isn’t aimed at children under 13, and I don’t knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
